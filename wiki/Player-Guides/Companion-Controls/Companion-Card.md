# Companion Inspect

Parent: [Companion Controls](/mod/alecs-tamework/companion-controls) | [Player Guides](/mod/alecs-tamework/player-guides)

Press **F** on a companion you own to open **Companion Inspect** beside your crosshair. The prompt reads "Press [F] to inspect". You do not need a command item.

## What Companion Inspect shows

- **Header**: the companion's name, species and gender. The pencil renames it, and the X closes the panel.
- **Info**: portrait, level, health, happiness, hunger, thirst and traits, as in the companion panel. Click the
  level, or the talent point badge, to open its talents.
- **Groups**: the picker under the portrait puts the companion in any of your groups. Create and edit groups in the
  companions window.
- **Mode**: one button per mode the companion supports, such as Follow, Hold and Idle. The current mode is
  highlighted; click another to switch.
- **Timers**: age stage, breeding cooldown and harvest timer, when the companion has them.
- **Actions**: shoulder, flight and breeding toggles, **Set Home** (here, where the companion stands) and
  **Return Home**, plus anything the companion's mod adds, such as **Ride** and **Pet**. A greyed-out button is not available right now, for example Ride on a dragon without the Flightmaster's
  Talisman in hand. Riding from the panel does not need crouching.
- **Footer**: **Release** and **Cull**, each with a confirmation.

Someone else's companion shows its info and owner only.

## Renaming

The pencil opens the naming window. Depending on the server, renaming needs a naming item such as a nametag
anywhere in your inventory (it is used up if the item says so), is free, or is turned off. The pencil is greyed out
when you need a naming item and have none.

## Choosing what F does

Open the companions window from your command item and click the gear to reach **Your settings**. Under **F on a
companion**, pick **Cycle modes** to have F cycle your companions' modes as before, **Inspect** to open
Companion Inspect, or **Server default** to follow the server's choice. Without a command item, use `/tw card cycle`,
`/tw card card` or `/tw card default`. If the server has locked this choice, the buttons are greyed out. Crouch + F still does what
it did, such as mounting. With Inspect chosen, F with an empty hand opens the panel instead of petting; use the panel's
Pet button.

Bonded companions, such as HyDragon's dragons, show their modes and their mod's buttons on the panel. Their other
actions stay in their own companion panel.

## Moving the panel

While Companion Inspect is open, the camera zooms in on the companion, and you stay where you stood. To keep your
normal camera instead, turn **Inspect camera** off in **Your settings**.

Companion Inspect opens just right of your crosshair. To put it somewhere else, open **Your settings** and click **Move** next
to **Companion Inspect**. Pick a snap point, nudge the sample into place and click Save. It opens there next time.
Reset puts it back beside the crosshair. Only your screen changes.
