# Companion Card

Parent: [Companion Controls](/mod/alecs-tamework/companion-controls) | [Player Guides](/mod/alecs-tamework/player-guides)

Press **F** on a companion you own to open its companion card beside your crosshair. You do not need a command item.

## What the card shows

- **Header**: the companion's name, species and gender. The pencil renames it, and the X closes the card.
- **Info**: portrait, level, health, happiness, hunger, thirst and traits, as in the companion panel. Click the
  level, or the talent point badge, to open its talents.
- **Mode**: one button per mode the companion supports, such as Follow, Hold and Idle. The current mode is
  highlighted; click another to switch.
- **Timers**: age stage, breeding cooldown and harvest timer, when the companion has them.
- **Actions**: shoulder, flight and breeding toggles, plus anything the companion's mod adds, such as **Ride** and
  **Pet**. A greyed-out button is not available right now, for example Ride on a dragon without the Flightmaster's
  Talisman in hand. Riding from the card does not need crouching.
- **Footer**: **Release** and **Cull**, each with a confirmation, and the **Open with F** switch.

Someone else's companion shows its info and owner only.

## Renaming

The pencil opens the naming window. Depending on the server, renaming needs a naming item such as a nametag
anywhere in your inventory (it is used up if the item says so), is free, or is turned off. The pencil is greyed out
when you need a naming item and have none.

## Choosing what F does

Turn **Open with F** off on the card, or use `/tw card cycle`, to have F cycle your companions' modes as before.
`/tw card card` turns the card back on, and `/tw card default` follows the server's choice. Crouch + F still does what
it did, such as mounting. With the card on, F with an empty hand opens the card instead of petting; use the card's
Pet button.

Bonded companions, such as HyDragon's dragons, show their modes and their mod's buttons on the card. Their other
actions stay in their own companion panel.
