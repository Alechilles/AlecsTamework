# Player Glossary

Parent: [Troubleshooting and Glossary](/mod/alecs-tamework/troubleshooting-and-glossary) | [Player Guides](/mod/alecs-tamework/player-guides)

## Common terms
- `Owner`: the player currently recognized as the companion's owner.
- `Tamed`: the companion is in the mod's controlled tame state rather than its wild state.
- `Linked`: the companion is recorded on a command tool.
- `Active` (tool): linked and included in normal bulk command dispatch.
- `Inactive` (tool): linked but excluded from bulk command dispatch until toggled back on.
- `NearbyMode`: panel mode that shows nearby eligible companions rather than only linked ones.
- `OwnedMode`: panel mode that shows all your owned animals across worlds and unloaded chunks, regardless of item links.
- `LinkedMode`: panel mode that shows companions linked to the current tool.
- `Set Home`: stores a home position for that companion.
- `Return Home`: sends the companion back to its stored home position.
- `Recall`: brings a living companion to you. On a captured card it takes the
  companion out of its capture item.
- `Attempting recall`: Tamework is loading the place where an unloaded
  companion was last seen. If the animal does not turn up before the countdown
  ends, the companion reappears next to you from its saved state.
- `Captured`: the companion is in a filled capture item. Release it through
  that item. If the item is gone, use `Recall` or `Forget` on its card.
- `Forget`: permanently gives up a captured companion and frees its ownership
  slot. Any surviving copy of its capture item becomes empty.
- `In Coop`: the companion is housed in a configured coop. It comes out when
  the coop lets its residents roam or when the coop block is broken.
- `Stored`: the companion is kept in a roster and has no animal in the world
  until you summon it.
- `Dead`: the companion died. Use `Revive`.
- `Revive`: brings back a dead companion when policy and any death cooldown
  allow it. It can require the exact item costs shown in the confirmation.
- `LOST`: the companion's animal is gone without a death, and its saved state
  was kept. Causes: the animal was removed from the world, its portal or
  instance world closed, its filled capture item despawned or fell out of the
  world, or it could not be found after an update from an older version. A
  companion is never `LOST` just because it is unloaded, far away, or a recall
  timed out.
- `Recover`: brings back a `LOST` companion from its saved state. It is free.
- `Being located after the update`: shown after a server update from an older
  Tamework version while Tamework looks for the companion's animal. Recall and
  Recover wait until it is found.
- `Companions owned`: every companion you own, wherever it is: out in the
  world, stored, in a capture item, in a coop, dead or `LOST`.
- `Companions out in the world`: your companions that have an animal in a
  world, loaded or not. Servers can limit this number separately.
- `Life Stage`: the creature's age phase, such as baby, adolescent, or adult.
- `Trait`: an inherited or assigned modifier that affects the companion's behavior or stats.
- `Needs`: recurring values such as hunger and thirst.
