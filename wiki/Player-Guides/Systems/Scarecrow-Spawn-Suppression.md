# Scarecrow Spawn Suppression

Parent: [Systems](/mod/alecs-tamework/systems) | [Player Guides](/mod/alecs-tamework/player-guides)

Place a Tamework scarecrow on a solid surface to pause automatic NPC spawn markers within an exact 32-block radius. Ordinary world spawning is suppressed in the native spawn chunks that intersect that area, so the horizontal edge can extend to a chunk boundary.

Use the placed scarecrow again to collect it. If your inventory is full, it stays in the world. Overlapping scarecrows each remain active until collected.

Scarecrows are interactable world props rather than blocks. Any nearby player can collect one unless the server's permissions cover custom entity interactions.

The scarecrow does not remove NPCs that already exist. It also does not block:

- manually triggered spawn markers;
- Tamework spawner items;
- commands, breeding, recalls, or other deliberate scripted spawns.

The current appearance is Hytale's stock scarecrow and is a temporary placeholder for a future custom model.
